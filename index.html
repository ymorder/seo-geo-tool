• OpenAI · gpt-4o-mini, gpt-4o, gpt-4-turbo, o1-mini
• Anthropic · claude-3-5-sonnet-latest, claude-3-5-haiku-latest
• Google Gemini · gemini-2.0-flash, gemini-1.5-flash (free tier with a key, much higher output limit)
