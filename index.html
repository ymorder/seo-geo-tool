Project Intake

Stage 1 · Competitor Research

Analyze top-ranking articles. Find gaps before you write a word.

What to deliver:
  • Top organic ranking articles for the primary keyword + close variations.
  • Keyword Map: primary keyword, secondary keywords, related entities, PAA questions.
  • Content Gaps: missing subtopics, weak logic, unverified data, missing risk factors or exceptions.

Stage 2 · GEO Prompts (AI Search)

Build quote-ready answers so ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI cite you.

Generate 10–15 realistic user prompts across styles: informational, how-to, best, comparison, definition. For each, write a 2–3 sentence direct answer optimized to be cited.

Stage 3 · Outline

Structure with asymmetry. Vary heading depths, list lengths, paragraph styles.

Structure: 1 H1 with primary keyword · brief direct intro · streamlined H2/H3 · FAQ (4–6 Qs) · conclusion · natural structural variation. No rigid template.

Stage 4 · Article Body

The publish-ready text. Follow every rule in the writing guidelines.

Writing rules:
  • Direct opening: answer the main query in the first 2–3 sentences. No filler.
  • H2 openings: 1–2 sentence direct answer before expanding.
  • Tone: expert, natural, human. No em dashes (—). Banned: delve, unlock, game changer, seamless, "in conclusion", לצלול, הסיבה לכך פשוטה.
  • Structural asymmetry: vary heading depths, list lengths, paragraph cadence.
  • Qualified statements: "depending on the framework", "subject to specific regulatory guidelines".
  • Strict language purity: pure character set, orthographic perfection, correct specialized terminology.

Stage 5 · Meta Tags & Slug

3 title options, 3 description options, 1 URL slug. Pick the recommended one.

Specs: Meta Title 50–60 chars · primary keyword near start · Meta Description 140–155 chars · factual value + soft CTA · URL slug hyphenated English.

Stage 6 · Featured Image

16:9 image generation prompt + alt text + filename.

Prompt must: 16:9 · realistic editorial photography OR modern abstract conceptual art · no text, no logos · specific subject + composition + lighting.

Stage 7 · Fact Check & Source Verification

Every claim, statistic, date and named entity verified against authoritative sources.

Stage 8 · Uniqueness & Math Audit

Confirm the text is unique and every number reconciles.

Stage 9 · Humanization & Style Verification

Sentence variety, natural transitions, no AI cadence residue.
